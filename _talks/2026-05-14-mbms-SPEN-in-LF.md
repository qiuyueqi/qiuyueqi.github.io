**Synopsis** <br> 
**Keywords**: Portable MRI, Diffusion-weighted imaging, SPEN

**Motivation**: EPI-based diffusion at low-field portable scanners suffers from severe field inhomogeneity-induced distortions, limited phase correction in multi-shot acquisitions due to few receive coils, and low SNR efficiency, which further constrains diffusion performance.

**Goal(s)**: To develop self-navigated multi-shot and RF-encoded multiband SPEN DWI methods that provide phase-corrected diffusion imaging with reduced distortion and enhanced SNR efficiency.

**Approach**: Multi-shot and multiband SPEN DWI sequences were implemented on a 110 mT portable MRI system, using SPEN's intrinsic self-navigation to correct motion-induced phase inconsistencies across shots and multiband dimensions.

**Results**: The proposed approach achieved phase-corrected, low-distortion DWI with improved SNR efficiency, without relying on multi-coil parallel imaging.

**Impact**: Self-navigated multi-shot SPEN DWI enables inter-shot phase correction with reduced distortion, while multiband SPEN DWI increases SNR efficiency. Together, these methods demonstrate phase-corrected, low-distortion diffusion imaging on portable low-field MRI without parallel imaging.
